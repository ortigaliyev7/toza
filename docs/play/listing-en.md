# Store listing — English

**Title (30):** Toza — messaging client

**Short description (80):** Unofficial Telegram client: deleted messages, cleanup tools, PIN lock

**Full description:**

Toza is a light, convenient client for your Telegram account. It uses Telegram's official protocol (MTProto): your number and login code go only to Telegram, and your session stays only on your phone.

⚠️ Toza is an unofficial app. It is built on the Telegram API but is not affiliated with or endorsed by Telegram.

Features:
• Deleted and edited messages: in private chats a deleted message stays in place and the previous text of edited messages is shown (on your device only).
• Clean up contacts, channels and groups: long-inactive and deleted accounts, unread channels.
• PIN lock and hidden chats.
• Latin ↔ Cyrillic converter, keyword alerts, quick-reply templates.
• Eye-care mode, a handy video player, smooth animations.
• Export chats to TXT, HTML, PDF or JSON.
• Encrypted backup: encrypted on your phone, we can't read it.

Toza Premium (optional subscription):
• Deleted and edited messages are kept even while Toza is closed (via Telegram Business; requires Telegram Premium).
• Basic: text and voice. Full: + photos and video messages.
• Monthly auto-renewing subscription. Cancel any time in Google Play → Subscriptions.

Monetization: the Toza Premium subscription only. No ads, no data selling.
Open source (GPL-3.0).
